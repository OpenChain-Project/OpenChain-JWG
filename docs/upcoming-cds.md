#### The 36th meeting / The 11th hybrid meeting

今回は OpenSSF との合同開催として、欧州で施行が進む欧州サイバーレジリエンス法（EU CRA: Cyber Resilience Act）への対応を中心に、SBOM、脆弱性管理、OSSガバナンスなどの実務的なテーマを取り上げます。
Day2では、デジタル庁のOSPOの取り組みや、生成AIとOSSコミュニティの関係など、今後のOSS活用を考える上で重要なテーマについて議論します。
OSSのコンプライアンスやセキュリティ業務の担当者だけでなく、 OSSを利用した製品・サービス開発に携わる方 ソフトウェア品質・セキュリティ担当者 OSPOや技術戦略に関心のある方 生成AIとOSSの関係に興味のある方 にもおすすめです。
初参加の方も歓迎します。
/
Organised jointly with OpenSSF, this event will focus on compliance with the European Union Cyber Resilience Act (EU CRA), which is currently being implemented across Europe, whilst also covering practical topics such as SBOMs, vulnerability management and OSS governance.
On Day 2, we will discuss key topics for considering the future use of OSS, such as the initiatives of the Digital Agency’s OSPO and the relationship between generative AI and the OSS community.
This event is recommended not only for those responsible for OSS compliance and security operations, but also for those involved in the development of products and services utilising OSS, software quality and security professionals, those interested in OSPO and technical strategy, and anyone curious about the relationship between generative AI and OSS.
First-time attendees are also welcome.
